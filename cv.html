Curriculum Vitae

Summary

  • Psychology Ph.D. Graduate, specialising in auditory perception, psychophysics, and human factors.
  • My research largely focused on understanding our perception of musical sound and how this applies to the design of safety-critical auditory alarms.
  • To this end, I have employed numerous cognitive experimental methods, and a wide range of quantitative methods to derive meaning from subsequent data.
  • I am now keen to transition into user experience based research and data science.

Hobbies and Interests

  • I am an advocate and enthusiast for public transport networks. I find interest in researching histories of various cities networks, and considering how the design promotes (or hinders) efficient movement of people.
  • I enjoy playing video games, particularly narrative driven role-play games and sandbox games. My favourite games include Baldur’s Gate 2: Shadows of Amn, LittleBigPlanet, and Minecraft. I have also written and programmed mods for various games.
